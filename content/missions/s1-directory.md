## The mission

Before a company goes public, it files an S-1 with the SEC. The S-1 says what the company does, how it makes money, and what can sink it. It is the most honest thing most companies write about themselves.

S1 Directory collects 258 of them, from tech IPOs between 1995 and 2018.

![The S1 Directory home page: a shelf of books with halftone covers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sjhangiani12/s1directory.com/HEAD/docs/hero-dark.jpg)

## What I built

- **The library.** Each filing is a book. Each cover is a public-domain artwork from the Art Institute of Chicago, printed as a two-color halftone. No two books share an artwork.
- **The books.** Each book has a short essay: what the company bet on, how the IPO went, and where the company is today.
- **The directory.** A list of all the filings, newest first, with search across names, tickers, founders, cities, and years.
- **Books that open.** Each cover sits on a hinge. Click a book, and the cover swings open before the page turns.

## History

I started the site in 2020 as a plain list of S-1 links. In 2026 I rebuilt it as The Opening Bell.
