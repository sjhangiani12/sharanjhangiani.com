## The mission

A good feature story shows three things at the same time: what the customer feels, what the product shows, and what the service does behind it.

## What I built

A storyboard shows one feature as a sequence of phases, for example Discovery, Onboarding, and First Use. Each phase has three frames.

| Frame | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Customer** | What the person does and feels. An illustration shows the scene. |
| **Product** | The screens that the person sees. |
| **Service** | The touchpoints and the processes behind the screens. |

Google Imagen draws each customer illustration from a short description of the scene. One style prompt applies to the full storyboard, so all the illustrations look alike.

## Status

Storyboard was an experiment from January 2026. I do not work on it now.
